About this tool
About RTSoftwares

A free, no-signup URL encoder and decoder. Percent-encode text or a query string (component, full-URL, form or every-character mode), decode it back (with "decode fully" for double-encoded URLs and a lenient fallback for broken sequences), or paste a URL to see its parts and edit its query parameters.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you paste is sent to a server.

URL Encoder & Decoder is built and maintained by RTSoftwares. We build small, free, no-signup web tools that run entirely in your browser.
